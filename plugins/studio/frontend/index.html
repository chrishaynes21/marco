Record a routine

  

    
Marco watches the keyboard and the mouse and writes down what you
      did, in order, with the real pauses. It does not look at the screen, so it works
      on menus nothing can recognise — and it replays exactly what you showed it.

      back and press Stop recording. Nothing is saved until you have looked at it
      and pressed Save.
